PRINCIPLES DESIGN RATIONALE

Three Foundational Decisions

01 // STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY

Elimination of Mechanical Screws

A standard drone houses between 35 and 60 miniature steel screws. Over prolonged high-RPM vibration regimes, these fasteners loosen, corrode, and introduce structural play. Gravitas unifies its airframe through precision co-cured carbon joints and interlocking titanium bayonet fittings, reducing mechanical fasteners to just four captive titanium bolts.

02 // THERMAL HOMEOSTASIS

Passive Conduction via Graphene

Motorized cooling fans suck in dust, moisture, and fine sea spray while consuming precious battery watts. Gravitas dissipates the thermal output of its 40 TOPS neural engine and radio amplifiers using pyrolytic graphene conduits that radiate heat uniformly across the entire carbon shell during flight airflow.

03 // KINETIC HARMONICS

Viscoelastic Gimbal Decoupling

Propeller revolutions inevitably introduce high-frequency motor harmonics. Rather than fighting vibrations algorithmically with software cropping, the full-frame optical gimbal hangs suspended inside four tuned fluoro-silicone viscoelastic dampers, ensuring that zero motor resonance ever reaches the optical sensor glass.
