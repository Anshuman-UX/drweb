Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our dual-use drone architecture, mechanical payload interfaces, cellular BVLOS ranges, and commercialization roadmap.

Conventional disaster drones sit in storage 95% of the time, causing crippling maintenance depreciation, while standard delivery drones cannot handle rough storm weather or winch drops. DRWEB AERO operates daily as a commercial B2B freight carrier generating consistent revenue, and instantly converts into a storm-hardened disaster rescue lifeline in under 2 minutes.
The UPI is a standardized electromechanical mounting plate located under the airframe belly. It incorporates high-amperage power bus contacts and high-speed data buses. An operator pulls a dual safety cam-lock latch, slides out the commercial cargo pod, slides in the motorized rescue winch or thermal camera, and re-engages the latch with zero tools required.
When winching down heavy supplies over floodwaters, pendulum swing creates violent weight shifts. Our customized flight controller runs high-frequency inertial observer loops that predict torque changes and instantaneously adjust Field-Oriented Control (FOC) motor RPMs to counteract aerodynamic destabilization in real time.
The AMR-PD heavy-lift platform supports up to 25 kg payload with a 1600mm wheelbase. Operational range is unbounded by visual line-of-sight since telemetry and 4K encrypted video stream over dual cellular 5G networks with satellite backup. Flight endurance ranges between 35 and 65 minutes depending on payload.
The avionics architecture features triple redundancy: dual independent carrier eSIMs (e.g. Jio and Airtel) plus Iridium satellite failover. If all carrier links disconnect, the onboard NVIDIA Jetson edge companion computer autonomously engages Visual-Inertial Odometry (VIO) to follow the pre-loaded mission flight corridor or executes an automated Return-to-Launch (RTL) safe landing.
Developed in compliance with the Ministry of Civil Aviation Drone Rules 2021 and Digital Sky NPNT (No Permission, No Takeoff) architecture. The platform has validated TRL 5 benchmarks at Incubatex Innovation Foundation, GIET University, with QCI type-certification trials underway in designated green-zone airspace corridors.
