Pursuit Along a Coastal Ridge Road

Following an endurance cyclist carving steep coastal hairpins at 72 km/h. EagleEye AI predicts brake entry points and sweeps low over sea cliffs, maintaining a steady 14-stop cinema frame with the ocean horizon locked rock-solid.

SCENARIO 02 / 04 GLACIAL CRYOSPHERE SURVEY
ALT: 3,400 M TEMP: -18°C GUSTS: 18.2 M/S

Alpine Crevasse Topographic Mapping

Operating in -18°C katabatic winds across a glacier field. The solid-state lithium-sulfur power cell sustains 46 minutes of survey transects, generating a sub-centimeter digital elevation model (DEM) of dynamic ice flow.

WIND FORCE: Beaufort 8 HEATING: Internal Graphene Jacket RTK ACCURACY: 0.8 cm
SCENARIO 03 / 04 OLD-GROWTH ECOLOGICAL CENSUS
CANOPY DEPTH: 45 M SOUND: 48.5 dBA GPS: UNAVAILABLE

Sub-Canopy Avian Observation

Weaving through dense rainforest undergrowth without GPS reception. OmniSight LiDAR creates an instantaneous 360° laser corridor while the WhisperDrive toroidal props operate so quietly that nesting raptors remain unperturbed just 6 meters away.

NAVIGATION: Pure Optical Flow & LiDAR ACOUSTICS: Below Leaf Rustle COLLISIONS: 0
SCENARIO 04 / 04 CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE AUDIT
ILLUMINATION: 0.02 LUX PROXIMITY: 0.8 M DEFECT: 0.4 MM RESOLVED

Midnight Suspension Bridge Cable Inspection

Hovering centimeters from high-voltage steel cables in pitch darkness. Garud’s dual native ISO sensor and millimeter ToF laser map stress fractures, micro-corrosion, and bolt loosening without requiring bridge lane closures.

SENSOR: Dual Base ISO 3200 EMC IMMUNITY: 50 kV/m Shielding DATA LINK: AES-256 Encrypted
AVIONICS OPERATIONAL FLIGHT MODES

Four Distinct Flight Regimes

MODE 01 // WHISPER CRUISE

Cinematic Whisper Mode

Limits rotor RPM acceleration spikes to prioritize absolute acoustic silence and ultra-smooth pan acceleration. Ideal for documentaries, nature reserves, and narrative cinema.

Max Speed: 8 m/s Noise: 44.2 dBA
MODE 02 // VECTOR PURSUIT

High-Speed Pursuit Mode

Unshackles full 24,000 RPM power to track vehicles, athletes, and watercraft. EagleEye AI predicts rapid turns and applies aggressive regenerative magnetic braking.

Max Speed: 25 m/s (90 km/h) Acceleration: 0–60 in 2.1s
MODE 03 // GEODETIC GRID

Precision Photogrammetry

Autonomous lawnmower grid execution with millimeter RTK waypoint precision. Triggers mechanical camera shutters with synchronized spatial coordinates and zero optical drift.

GSD: 0.4 cm/px at 80m Overlap: 85% Front / 75% Side
MODE 04 // CONFINED STEALTH

Subterranean & Indoor

Disengages satellite dependence entirely. Navigates tight mining shafts, subway tunnels, and building interiors using omnidirectional LiDAR and 300 Hz downward infrared flow.

GPS: Disengaged LiDAR Barrier: 0.5m Safe Cushion
