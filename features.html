CATALOGUE // CHAPTER 02 TECHNICAL SUBSYSTEMS

Eight Core Disciplines

Every sub-assembly of Gravitas was engineered from first physical principles to eliminate mechanical clutter and acoustic turbulence. Below is the complete archival index of the eight integrated feature systems.

01 // OPTICS ±0.005° STABILITY

Full-Frame 3-Axis Precision Gimbal

Back-illuminated full-frame optical sensor mounted on a 3-axis brushless magnesium gimbal, capturing 8K 30fps and 4K 120fps video across 14 stops of uncompressed dynamic range.

14 STOPS DYNAMIC RANGE
4K 120P CINEMA RAW
Why it matters: Delivers master-grade cinema cinematography free from digital cropping, micro-jitter, or rolling shutter artifacts.
View Camera Monograph →
02 // KINETICS 48.5 dBA SILENCE

WhisperDrive™ Toroidal Propulsion

Continuous carbon-fiber toroidal loop rotors eliminate open blade tips where vortex shedding creates high-pitch scream, operating 60% quieter than conventional open-blade quadcopters.

48.5 dBA NOISE AT 15M
24,000 PEAK RPM
Why it matters: Permits unobtrusive flights over pristine wildlife habitats, sensitive livestock pastures, and acoustically restricted architectural environments.
View Propulsion Monograph →
03 // POWER 52 MIN HOVER

Solid-State Lithium-Sulfur Power Cell

Non-flammable solid electrolyte pouch cells delivering 410 Wh/kg specific energy density, maintaining continuous hover for 52 minutes and functioning reliably in extreme thermal brackets.

52 MIN ENDURANCE
-25° TO +55°C THERMAL SPAN
Why it matters: Eliminates battery anxiety during critical scientific transects and search operations without cold-weather voltage drops.
View Battery Monograph →
04 // TELEMETRY 360° OMNI SHIELD

OmniSight™ Hex-Directional LiDAR

Six integrated time-of-flight solid-state laser transceivers projecting 120,000 pulses per second to synthesize a 45-meter spherical barrier against wires, branches, and obstacles.

45 METERS SCAN RADIUS
4 MS REACTION TIME
Why it matters: Guarantees crash avoidance even in unmapped pitch-black interior shafts, foggy valleys, or dense tree canopies.
View Sensors Monograph →
05 // AVIONICS 18 KM TELEMETRY

GravitasLink Ultra-Spectral Transmission

Tri-band SDR (Software-Defined Radio) running at 2.4 GHz, 5.8 GHz, and 868 MHz with frequency hopping, streaming 1080p 60fps feed with 18ms latency encrypted via AES-256.

18 KM LOS DISTANCE
18 MS FRAME LATENCY
Why it matters: Ensures continuous pilot authority and uncompressed broadcast monitoring through complex urban RF interference.
View Transmission Monograph →
06 // STRUCTURE 249g / FORCE 8 GALE

Carbon-Titanium Monocoque Skeleton

Continuous hollow monocoque molded from Toray T1000 carbon fiber bonded with 3D-printed Grade 5 titanium stress nodes, withstanding Force 8 gales (18 m/s) at only 249 grams.

249 G MASS CATEGORY
18 M/S GALE RESISTANCE
Why it matters: Fits under global transport licensing limits while providing the structural stiffness of heavy industrial aircraft.
View Airframe Monograph →
07 // COGNITION 40 TOPS AI CORE

EagleEye AI Autonomous Tracking Core

Onboard edge neural processor executing 40 trillion operations per second, executing real-time kinematic vector prediction and re-acquiring occluded targets in 0.03 seconds.

40 TOPS EDGE COMPUTE
0.03 SEC RE-ACQUISITION
Why it matters: Enables solo documentary operators to capture dynamic high-speed sports and vehicular motion without a secondary camera operator.
View Tracking Monograph →
08 // GEODETICS < 1.5 CM PERCH PRECISION

Precision Return-to-Perch Navigation

Multi-constellation dual-band RTK satellite receiver fused with downward visual stereoscopic odometry, touching down on charging perches with sub-centimeter repeatability.

< 1.5 CM LANDING ACCURACY
DUAL RTK L1 / L5 BANDS
Why it matters: Permits unattended automated station dockings on moving research vessels, communication towers, and field rovers.
View Navigation Monograph →
