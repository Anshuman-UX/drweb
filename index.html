MANIFESTO DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

An aerial instrument born at the convergence of natural raptorial acuity and acoustic tranquility.

For decades, low-altitude flight has been burdened by intrusive acoustic friction, fragile multi-part assemblies, and compromised sensor payloads. Garud dismantles this legacy through unified monocoque engineering and quiet toroidal fluid mechanics.

The mythic eagle possessed an eye capable of dissecting terrain across leagues of distance while riding the air without perceptible exertion. Garud honors this lineage not through ornamentation, but through engineering integrity: an ultra-rigid carbon-titanium frame weighing 249 grams, a 14-stop optical core, and omni-directional solid-state LiDAR that renders airspace transparent.

FEATURE 01 / 08 OPTICAL STABILIZATION

Full-Frame 3-Axis Precision Gimbal

A custom-milled aerospace magnesium gimbal housing holding a back-illuminated full-frame sensor capable of resolving cinematic 8K at 30 frames and high-speed 4K at 120 frames per second.

Angular Jitter Variance ±0.005°
Dynamic Range 14 Stops (Dual Native ISO)
Video Formats 10-Bit 4:2:2 D-Log / ProRes RAW
WHY IT MATTERS

Eliminates micro-vibrations without algorithmic digital crop, preserving edge-to-edge optical fidelity even during high-velocity maneuvers in turbulent alpine crosswinds.

FEATURE 02 / 08 KINETIC ACOUSTICS

WhisperDrive™ Toroidal Propulsion

Closed-loop toroidal rotor blades manufactured from continuous carbon filaments, eliminating open blade tips where high-frequency turbulence vortices generate harsh motor whine.

Acoustic Output 48.5 dBA at 15m (60% quieter)
Peak Motor Velocity 24,000 RPM (Brushless FOC)
Thrust Efficiency 11.4 g/W Peak
WHY IT MATTERS

Wildlife researchers, documentarians, and urban surveyors can operate within close proximity of sensitive subjects without disturbing natural ecosystems or violating urban acoustic zoning.

FEATURE 03 / 08 SPATIAL TELEMETRY

OmniSight™ Hex-Directional LiDAR

Six solid-state time-of-flight laser arrays scanning a 360-degree perimeter shield, building an instantaneous millimeter-accurate point cloud of complex physical environments in total darkness.

Detection Range 45 Meters (Spherical 360°)
Sample Acquisition 120,000 Points / Second
Response Latency 4 Milliseconds
WHY IT MATters

Guarantees autonomous collision immunity through dense forest canopies, unmapped industrial infrastructure, and confined subterranean mine shafts without relying on GPS.
