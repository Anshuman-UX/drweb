ARCHITECTURAL BREAKTHROUGHS

Built to Conquer the Dual-Use Dilemma

Traditional disaster drones sit idle in warehouses draining taxpayer funds. We designed a modular aerial chassis that earns its keep daily.

Dual-Revenue Economic Model

Operates daily as a high-margin commercial B2B freight carrier for hospital pharma and third-party logistics. When floods strike, the fully funded active fleet mobilizes instantly for search-and-rescue.

Peacetime & Crisis

Universal Quick-Release Rail (UPI)

Standardized electromechanical coupling with high-current pass-through. Swap a rigid commercial cargo container for a motorized life-vest winch or thermal search pod in under 120 seconds with zero tools.

< 2 Min Swap

Active CoG Trim Algorithms

Dynamic center-of-gravity compensation algorithms instantly stabilize the airframe during turbulent in-flight payload winch drops over submerged terrain and high-wind shear.

FOC Motor Control

Cellular BVLOS Infinite Range

Integrated 4G/5G transceiver architecture paired with omnidirectional arrays. Control missions from hundreds of kilometers away via national cellular footprints rather than 5 km radio-range limits.

SIM-Enabled Telemetry

NVIDIA Jetson Edge Autonomy

Onboard companion computer executes real-time neural networks for victim detection, obstacle avoidance, visual-inertial odometry, and airborne radio relay when ground cellular towers collapse.

Edge AI Computer Vision

Redundant Avionics & Failsafe

Dual triple-axis IMUs, multi-constellation GNSS (GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou), barometric altimeters, smart lithium BMS cell-balancing, and terrain-aware emergency safe landing protocols.
