FEATURE 04 / 08 SOLID-STATE SENSORS & SPATIAL SHIELD

OmniSight™ Hex-Directional LiDAR

Six micro-solid-state time-of-flight laser emitters scanning 120,000 points per second across a 45-meter perimeter. In pitch-black darkness, heavy fog, or reflective glass corridors, Gravitas perceives physical reality in three unblinking dimensions.

360° SPHERICAL FIELD OF PERCEPTION
45 M DETECTION HORIZON
120k POINTS ACQUIRED / SECOND
4 MS OBSTACLE REFLEX LATENCY

01 // MILLIMETER-SCALE SPATIAL SYNTHESIS

Vision-only obstacle systems blind easily in direct sunlight glare and become completely helpless in darkness or fog. Gravitas utilizes solid-state 905nm infrared laser arrays embedded directly into the perimeter nodes of the carbon fuselage. By measuring light travel time down to picoseconds, the flight computer constructs an instantaneous spatial mesh that detects thin utility wires, tree branches, and dynamic hazards long before visual cameras can discern them.

ARRAY ARCHITECTURE: 6X PERIMETER SOLID-STATE APERTURES REFRESH RATE: 100 HZ CONTINUOUS SWEEP

Schematic: Hex-directional geometry ensures zero blind spots across both horizontal and vertical quadrants.

02 // VERIFIABLE TECHNICAL DATA

Sensor Modality Hex-Directional Solid-State Time-of-Flight (ToF) LiDAR
Laser Wavelength 905 nm Class 1 (Eye-Safe under IEC 60825-1)
Point Cloud Density 120,000 points / second across 360° × 180° sphere
Minimum Resolvable Target 2 mm diameter telephone line at 12 meters distance
Reaction Reflex 4 ms path deviation computation on edge FPGA

03 // WHY IT MATTERS TO THE USER

The fear of catastrophic collisions causes operators to fly conservatively, avoiding complex environments and missing critical camera angles.

OmniSight™ liberates the operator. Gravitas navigates narrow architectural lightwells, weaves between forest vines, and flies confidently through unlit cavern tunnels with absolute autonomous safety margins.
