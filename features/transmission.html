FEATURE 05 / 08 TELECOMMUNICATIONS & CRYPTOGRAPHY

GravitasLink Ultra-Spectral Transmission

Software-defined radio across 2.4 GHz, 5.8 GHz, and sub-gigahertz 868 MHz bands. Gravitas streams crystal-clear 1080p 60fps monitor feeds over 18 kilometers of open airspace with an imperceptible 18-millisecond glass-to-glass latency.

18 KM MAX LINE-OF-SIGHT REACH
18 MS END-TO-END FEED LATENCY
TRI-BAND 2.4G / 5.8G / 868 MHz SDR
AES-256 HARDWARE ENCRYPTED LINK

01 // ADAPTIVE COGNITIVE HOPPING

In dense metropolitan hubs or around broadcast transmission towers, radio spectrum is an aggressive battleground of electromagnetic interference. GravitasLink continuously monitors 48 discrete channel frequencies per second. The moment noise spikes in one frequency slice, packet delivery routes instantaneously to clear spectrum without a single dropped frame.

ANTENNA INTEGRATION: DORSAL & VENTRAL DIPOLE ARRAYS EMC SHIELDING: COPPER-NICKEL NANO-MESH

Schematic: Conformal antennas embedded directly inside the airframe matrix eliminate fragile external plastic stalks.

02 // VERIFIABLE TECHNICAL DATA

Operating Frequencies 2.400–2.4835 GHz / 5.725–5.850 GHz / 868–915 MHz (ISM & SRD)
Maximum LOS Range 18 km (FCC / Global Open Band) • 12 km (CE Compliance)
Live Monitor Resolution 1080p at 60 fps (H.265 hardware decoded)
Glass-to-Glass Latency 18 milliseconds (Direct Silicon-to-Display Pipeline)
Encryption Standard FIPS 140-2 Level 3 compliant AES-256 hardware cipher

03 // WHY IT MATTERS TO THE USER

Loss of telemetry during complex operations produces immediate panic and compromises mission critical objectives.

With GravitasLink's 18-kilometer envelope and resilient frequency-hopping architecture, operators maintain complete pilot authority and pristine live preview feeds through steel bridges, forested valleys, and radio-congested ports.
