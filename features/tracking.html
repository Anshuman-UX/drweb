FEATURE 07 / 08 EDGE COGNITION & KINEMATIC PREDICTION

EagleEye AI Autonomous Tracking Core

An edge neural inference accelerator executing 40 trillion operations per second. EagleEye fuses visual optical flow with high-speed LiDAR vectors, predicting subject trajectory through dense obstacles and re-locking onto obscured targets within 0.03 seconds.

40 TOPS EDGE NEURAL PROCESSING POWER
0.03 s OCCLUSION RE-ACQUISITION TIME
120 km/h MAX SUBJECT TRACKING VELOCITY
3D POSE REAL-TIME SKELETAL PREDICTION

01 // PREDICTIVE TRAJECTORY MODELING

Standard tracking algorithms fail the moment an athlete ducks behind a tree or a speeding rally car plunges through a cloud of dust. EagleEye computes continuous probabilistic kinematics. It understands human skeletal articulation, vehicular braking envelopes, and animal herd vectors, ensuring that when the subject re-emerges, Gravitas's gimbal is already pointed precisely at the predicted exit vector.

02 // VERIFIABLE TECHNICAL DATA

Neural Compute Performance 40 TOPS (INT8 / FP16 mixed precision matrix tensor core)
Target Acquisition Speed 0.03 seconds from occlusion reappearance to gimbal tracking lock
Maximum Tracking Speed 120 km/h (Tested with sports vehicles and downhill mountain bikers)
Simultaneous Track Capacity Tracks up to 16 discrete subjects with real-time bounding box depth
Power Consumption 6.2 Watts average at peak neural workload

03 // WHY IT MATTERS TO THE USER

Capturing high-speed kinetic action usually mandates a dedicated two-person crew: a master pilot and a separate camera operator holding a heavy gimbal remote.

EagleEye AI acts as an autonomous virtual director. Solo soloists, documentary crews, and research scientists simply select their subject on the monitor; Gravitas executes cinematic parallax moves, high-speed fly-bys, and continuous framing autonomously.
