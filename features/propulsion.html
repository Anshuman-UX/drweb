FEATURE 02 / 08 AERODYNAMICS & ACOUSTIC ARCHITECTURE

WhisperDrive™ Toroidal Propulsion

Closed-loop toroidal rotor rings fabricated from unbroken pre-preg carbon fibers. By eliminating open blade tips, Garud dissolves aerodynamic tip vortices, yielding a 48.5 dBA sound signature that vanishes into natural ambient rustle.

48.5 dBA ACOUSTIC LEVEL AT 15M
-60% ACOUSTIC ENERGY REDUCTION
24,000 PEAK RPM (3-PHASE FOC)
11.4 g/W SPECIFIC THRUST EFFICIENCY

01 // THE FLUID DYNAMICS OF SILENCE

Traditional drone propellers produce their distinctive, irritating whine because air spills violently across the high-velocity blade tip, shedding micro-tornados into the air stream. The WhisperDrive™ architecture curves each blade back into itself in a seamless toroidal loop. Airflow is contained, distributed, and released with laminar gentleness.

PROPULSION DATUM: 4X RADIAL TOROIDAL HUBS STATOR: 12-POLE SINTERED NEODYMIUM N52H

Schematic: Toroidal rotor geometry dispersing high-frequency vortex harmonics into broad sub-audible pink noise.

02 // VERIFIABLE TECHNICAL DATA

Acoustic Signature 48.5 dBA at 15m radius (Equivalent to a quiet residential library)
Frequency Distribution Peak shifted below 350 Hz; eliminated typical 2–5 kHz blade whine
Motor Architecture Custom 1404 outrunner with 0.15mm silicon steel laminations
Electronic Speed Control 32-bit dual FOC (Field Oriented Control) running at 96 kHz PWM
Dynamic Braking Regenerative active-braking reclaiming up to 4.2% kinetic descent energy

03 // WHY IT MATTERS TO THE USER

Acoustic intrusion is the primary barrier preventing aerial cinematography and survey work in ecologically sensitive biomes.

With WhisperDrive™, researchers observe nesting raptors and herd migrations without inciting panic or scattering subjects. In municipal settings, Garud operates without drawing the ire of bystanders or triggering ambient noise complaints.
