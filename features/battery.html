FEATURE 03 / 08 ELECTROCHEMISTRY & THERMAL EQUILIBRIUM

Solid-State Lithium-Sulfur Power Cell

A radical departure from unstable liquid-electrolyte lithium-ion cells. Gravitas utilizes a solid ceramic-sulfur matrix delivering 410 Wh/kg gravimetric energy density, granting 52 minutes of uninterrupted aloft flight in temperatures ranging from -25°C to +55°C.

52 MIN CONTINUOUS HOVER TIME
410 Wh/kg SPECIFIC GRAVIMETRIC DENSITY
-25° / +55°C OPERATING TEMPERATURE SPAN
1,200 CYCLES TO 85% RETENTION

01 // SOLID-STATE ELECTROLYTE MATRIX

Traditional drone batteries depend on volatile organic liquid solvents that degrade rapidly in cold air and pose catastrophic fire risks upon high-impact shock. Gravitas replaces liquid electrolytes with an ultra-thin, non-flammable ceramic polymer separator. By integrating a nano-structured sulfur cathode, the cell achieves nearly double the energy capacity of conventional LiPo packs without adding dead weight.

CHASSIS CORE DATUM: CENTER-OF-GRAVITY BAY STRUCTURAL INTEGRATION: MONOCOQUE LOAD-BEARING

Schematic: The power cell forms a structural shear-web inside the carbon monocoque, optimizing mass distribution at the center of gravity.

02 // VERIFIABLE TECHNICAL DATA

Cell Chemistry Solid-State Lithium-Sulfur (Li-S) pouch with ceramic solid electrolyte
Gravimetric Energy Density 410 Wh/kg (vs. standard drone LiPo at ~220 Wh/kg)
Operational Endurance 52 min hover / 46 min continuous cruise at 15 m/s
Operating Temperature -25°C to +55°C with internal graphene thermo-conductive jacket
Charging Protocol GaN Ultra-Fast Direct Perch Coupling (0% to 80% in 22 minutes)

03 // WHY IT MATTERS TO THE USER

Short 20-minute flight ceilings turn aerial missions into stressful races against low-battery warnings.

With 52 minutes of uninterrupted endurance, pilots and autonomous research grids execute complete multi-kilometer landscape sweeps on a single charge. Scientific expeditions into sub-zero glacial terrain operate without the sudden voltage collapse that commonly grounds legacy drones.
