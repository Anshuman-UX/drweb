FEATURE 08 / 08 GEODETICS & AUTONOMOUS TERMINAL GUIDANCE

Precision Return-to-Perch Navigation

Dual-frequency multi-constellation RTK geodetics fused with downward infrared visual odometry. When battery thresholds or mission parameters trigger a return, Gravitas settles onto its charging perch with sub-1.5 centimeter physical repeatability.

< 1.5 cm TERMINAL LANDING REPEATABILITY
DUAL RTK L1/L5 GPS, GLONASS, GALILEO, BEIDOU
300 HZ OPTICAL FLOW VELOCIMETRY
ZERO GPS AUTONOMOUS INDOOR HOVER LOCK

01 // MILLIMETRIC GEODETIC FUSION

Standard commercial GPS drifts by up to three meters, rendering automated nest or docking box landings unreliable in gusty winds. Gravitas incorporates an L1/L5 dual-band RTK engine that corrects ionospheric delay in real time. As Gravitas enters its terminal descent phase, high-speed ventral infrared cameras lock onto the microscopic fiducial matrix etched into the landing perch, guiding magnetic charging pads together without human intervention.

02 // VERIFIABLE TECHNICAL DATA

GNSS Constellations GPS (L1C/A, L5), GLONASS (L1OF), Galileo (E1, E5a), BeiDou (B1I, B2a)
RTK Accuracy Horizon Horizontal: 0.8 cm + 1 ppm // Vertical: 1.5 cm + 1 ppm
Optical Odometry Frequency 300 Hz downward infrared monocular visual flow sensor
Moving Deck Compatibility Tracks and lands on marine vessels rolling up to ±12° in open seas
Perch Docking Interface Neodymium magnetic kinetic coupling with gold-plated Pogo pins

03 // WHY IT MATTERS TO THE USER

Automated workflows collapse if aircraft require manual retrieval and plug-in tethering after every flight.

With Precision Return-to-Perch, Gravitas can be permanently stationed on remote scientific observation towers, agricultural sheds, or marine survey ships, autonomously cycling flights and recharges for months without human hands touching the airframe.
