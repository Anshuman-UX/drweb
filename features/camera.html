FEATURE 01 / 08 OPTICAL KINEMATICS & SENSOR CORE

Full-Frame 3-Axis Precision Gimbal

A bespoke optoelectronic core pairing a back-illuminated full-frame cinema sensor with an aerospace-grade direct-drive magnesium gimbal, capturing true 8K at 30fps and 4K at 120fps with ±0.005° mechanical jitter stability.

01 // WHAT IT DOES IN PLAIN WORDS

The camera does not merely stabilize an image electronically through cropped pixels; it floats on three frictionless brushless magnetic gimbals tuned to counteract sudden air gusts, motor harmonics, and aggressive high-speed vehicle maneuvers. The full-frame sensor swallows vast amounts of light, retaining delicate highlight detail in blinding alpine snow while simultaneously preserving shadow textures in dense forest floors.

02 // VERIFIABLE TECHNICAL DATA

Sensor Dimension 35.8 × 23.9 mm (3:2 Full-Frame BSI CMOS)

Angular Stability ±0.005° (Mechanical Feedback at 2,000 Hz)
Native ISO Ceiling Dual Base: ISO 800 and ISO 3200 (Clean up to 12,800)
Dynamic Range 14.2 verified stops via calibrated Stouffer step chart
Lens Integration Equivalent 24mm f/1.8 custom aspherical 9-element optic

03 // WHY IT MATTERS TO THE USER

For cinematographers and scientific surveyors alike, software stabilization is a compromise that crops pixels and introduces gelatinous motion blur.

By resolving optical stability mechanically at the hardware level, Gravitas outputs clean, broadcast-ready footage that matches high-end ground cameras. Surveyors receive distortion-free orthomosaic tiles where individual leaves, structural weld cracks, or geological fissures remain crisp down to sub-millimeter ground sampling distances.
