FEATURE 06 / 08 MATERIALS SCIENCE & MONOCOQUE TOPOLOGY

Carbon-Titanium Monocoque Skeleton

A single unified hollow fuselage molded from Toray T1000 carbon fiber and reinforced with generative Grade 5 titanium structural inserts. Delivering uncompromised Force 8 gale (18 m/s) wind resistance while registering exactly 249 grams on the analytical balance.

249 g TOTAL ALL-UP FLYING WEIGHT
18 m/s WIND GUST RESISTANCE (FORCE 8)
T1000 INTERMEDIATE MODULUS CARBON
Ti-6Al-4V DMLS 3D PRINTED TITANIUM NODES

01 // STRUCTURAL UNIFICATION WITHOUT MECHANICAL FASTENERS

Most quadcopters are assembled from dozens of injection-molded plastic shells held together by steel screws that rattle loose over hundreds of vibration cycles. Gravitas is cured as a continuous hollow monocoque inside a high-pressure autoclave. The fuselage itself acts as the primary load path, heat sink, and electronic enclosure, eliminating over 40 redundant mechanical fasteners.

MONOCOQUE DATUM: CONTINUOUS TOROIDAL SPARS AUTOCLAVE CURE: 180°C AT 7 BAR PRESSURE

02 // VERIFIABLE TECHNICAL DATA

Carbon Weave Specification Torayca® T1000G Intermediate Modulus (Tensile Strength: 6,370 MPa)
Metallic Reinforcement Selective Laser Sintered (SLS) Grade 5 Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V)
Takeoff Weight (TOW) 249.0 grams ± 0.5g (Sub-250g global aviation category compliant)
Maximum Wind Resistance 18.0 m/s sustained gale (Force 8 on the Beaufort scale)
Torsional Rigidity 14.8 Nm/deg (3.4× stiffer than aluminum alloy frames)

03 // WHY IT MATTERS TO THE USER

Aviation regulations worldwide impose arduous registration, licensing, and flight authorization barriers on drones weighing 250 grams or more.

By landing at precisely 249 grams without sacrificing structural stiffness or sensor size, Gravitas can be transported and operated internationally with maximum regulatory freedom while retaining the rough-weather stability of high-end commercial airframes.
