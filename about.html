FOUNDATION & VISION

Solving the Dual-Use Dilemma in Aerial Robotics

Born at Incubatex Innovation Foundation, GIET University (Gunupur-765022). We are bridging the gap between high-frequency commercial logistics and mission-critical humanitarian response through open-architecture heavy-lift UAS.

THE CORE BOTTLENECK

Why Traditional Drone Fleets Fail

1. The Financial Drain

Specialized disaster-relief drones sit idle 95% of the year in municipal storage, burning budgets through maintenance depreciation and pilot unreadiness without yielding operational ROI.

2. The Hardware Limitation

Commercial delivery drones are engineered strictly for rigid boxes in clear weather. They lack weatherproofing, dynamic center-of-gravity compensation, and motorized winches needed over raging floodwaters.

3. The Missing Link

Organizations are forced into purchasing two separate costly fleets. Until DRWEB AERO, there was no single heavy-lift platform that pays for itself through daily cargo and instantly converts into a flood lifeline.

DEEP TECH ARCHITECTURE

Six Primary Industrial Layers

Industrial-grade avionics, propulsion, and edge compute designed for endurance and hostile monsoon environments.

1. Heterogeneous Edge Compute Node

Powered by NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano companion computing. Executes real-time sensor fusion, automated visual-inertial odometry (VIO), and fail-safe decision logic without cloud dependency.

2. Cellular BVLOS Telemetry Gateway

Multi-band 4G/5G transceiver pairing with eSIM architecture and high-gain omnidirectional antenna arrays. Delivers unbounded transmission ranges across national cellular footprints.

3. Industrial Heavy-Lift Brushless Powertrain

High-torque BLDC motors paired with Field-Oriented Control (FOC) Electronic Speed Controllers and 30-inch carbon composite propellers for high dynamic throttle response in gale wind-shear.

4. Dual-Redundant Flight Control & Sensors

Pixhawk Cube Orange+ architecture integrating multi-constellation GNSS (GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou) with dual triple-axis IMUs and real-time dynamic Center of Gravity (CoG) auto-trim routines.

5. Universal Quick-Release Payload Interface (UPI)

Modular power-and-data coupling featuring high-current pass-through lines, quick-locking mechanical latching, and direct bus communication for instant module interchange under 2 minutes.

6. Intelligent High-Density Power Management

14S high-discharge lithium architecture governed by an active Battery Management System (BMS) with cell balancing, voltage surge suppression, thermal telemetry, and automated low-power RTL.

COMMERCIALIZATION ROADMAP

From TRL 5 Validation to Fleet Dominance

A phased, capital-efficient trajectory capitalizing on validated software autonomy.

PHASE 1: MONTHS 1–4

Heavy-Lift Integration & TRL 7 Field Trials

Mounting validated edge-compute and cellular telemetry onto the 1600mm hexacopter chassis. Stress testing the 15-kg payload winch and confirming dynamic CoG compensation during mid-air drop maneuvers.

PHASE 2: MONTHS 5–8

DGCA Type Certification & Cellular Sandbox

Quality Council of India (QCI) compliance testing for commercial DGCA certification. Executing closed-loop hospital pharma courier routes and simulated flood-relief drills with State Disaster Authorities (SDMA).

PHASE 3: MONTHS 9–12

Go-to-Market & B2G Readiness Retainers

Rollout of commercial DaaS (Drone-as-a-Service) middle-mile freight routes generating daily baseline cashflow. Securing municipal standby readiness retainers ahead of the annual monsoon season.

PHASE 4: YEAR 2+

Autonomous Swarms & Drone-in-a-Box (DiaB)

Scaling airframe to 50–100 kg ultra-heavy payloads with Hydrogen Fuel Cell hybrid propulsion. Rolling out 24/7 automated recharging ground stations and multi-agent grid search algorithms.
